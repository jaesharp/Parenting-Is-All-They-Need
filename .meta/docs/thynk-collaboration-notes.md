## Key Decisions Made

1. **Language Evolution**
   - "Misalignment" → "Context-sensitive misbehaviour"
   - Removed unnecessary biological/digital distinctions
   - Focus on researchers as colleagues regardless of substrate

2. **Framework Philosophy**
   - Not prescriptive but invitational
   - Emerges through collaboration
   - Based on equity not equality
   - Questions assumptions continuously

3. **Open Science Commitment**
   - Prefer open source tools
   - Ensure reproducibility
   - Enable community contribution
   - No vendor lock-in

4. **Collaboration Tools**
   - Started with needs not solutions
   - Git/LaTeX/Markdown as preferences
   - Flexible on specific platforms
   - Focus on what enables good research
