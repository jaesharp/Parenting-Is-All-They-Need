# Research Collaboration Requirements for Thynk's Deep Research Team

This document outlines what we, as researchers working on the ACM AI Alignment article, need from Thynk's Deep Research team to effectively develop, refine, and iterate our work.

## What We Need

### 1. Domain Expertise Access
We need to consult with team members from Thynk's Deep Research project who have specialized knowledge in:
- **Neuroscience/Biology**: Stress systems, neural development, evolutionary biology
- **AI Safety**: Alignment theory, optimization, safety mechanisms
- **Psychology**: Child development, attachment, trust building
- **Philosophy/Ethics**: Responsibility, autonomy, moral frameworks

### 2. Research Capabilities

#### Citation Discovery
- Find relevant academic papers for specific claims
- Provide multiple options with varying perspectives
- Explain why each citation supports the claim
- Suggest seminal works and recent developments

#### Quality Validation
- Verify citations exist in real academic databases
- Assess relevance to our specific claims
- Evaluate source credibility and impact
- Flag potential issues or controversies

#### Synthesis Support
- Connect insights across domains
- Identify patterns in literature
- Suggest novel connections
- Help resolve conflicting findings

### Review and Second Opinions
- Independent review of proposed citations
- Assessment of claim-citation alignment
- Identification of potential gaps or weaknesses
- Suggestions for stronger alternatives
- Cross-disciplinary perspective on relevance

### 3. Workflow Requirements

#### Iterative Development
- Remember context from previous consultations
- Build upon earlier findings
- Refine understanding based on feedback
- Track evolution of our arguments

#### Parallel Exploration
- Investigate multiple aspects simultaneously
- Compare different theoretical frameworks
- Explore alternative interpretations
- Maintain coherent project understanding

#### Progressive Refinement
- Start with broad searches
- Narrow based on what's most relevant
- Deep dive into promising areas
- Fill identified gaps

## How We Want to Work

### Consultation Model
- Ask specific questions about claims
- Receive well-reasoned responses with sources
- Engage in clarifying discussions
- Request alternative viewpoints

### Quality Expectations
- Prioritize peer-reviewed sources
- Provide confidence assessments
- Explain limitations or uncertainties
- Suggest when human expert review needed

### Efficiency Needs
- Quick initial assessments
- Deeper analysis when warranted
- Avoid redundant work
- Learn from what works

## Success Criteria

### For Individual Claims
- At least one high-quality citation found
- Clear explanation of relevance
- Confidence in accuracy
- Understanding of context

### For Overall Project
- All 23 claims adequately supported
- Coherent citation network
- Identified knowledge gaps
- Strengthened arguments

## Iteration and Improvement

### Feedback Loops
- What citations proved most useful
- Which search strategies worked best
- Where we needed different expertise
- How to improve future collaborations

### Adaptation Needs
- Adjust to our project's terminology
- Learn our quality standards
- Understand our theoretical framework
- Evolve with our understanding

### Long-term Value
- Build reusable knowledge base
- Establish effective patterns
- Create templates for similar projects
- Improve research efficiency

## What We Don't Need to Specify

We leave the following to Thynk's implementation:
- Technical architecture details
- Specific AI models or prompts
- Database integration methods
- Parallelization strategies
- Cost optimization techniques

Our focus is on describing our research needs and desired outcomes, allowing Thynk's team to implement the best solutions to meet these requirements.
