# Thynk Institute Deep Research Team Structure

This document outlines the team structure and roles within Thynk Institute's Deep Research project that we'll be collaborating with.

## Team Overview

The Deep Research project at Thynk Institute features research professionals who bring domain expertise and academic rigor to their respective fields.

## Team Roles

### Research Staff
- **Senior Research Specialists**: Lead investigators with deep domain expertise
- **Research Associates**: Specialists conducting focused investigations
- **Research Analysts**: Supporting research and initial explorations

### Information Science Team
- **Information Retrieval Specialists**: Expert database and literature searchers
- **Data Librarians**: Curating and organizing research resources
- **Citation Analysts**: Verifying and evaluating source quality

### Support Staff
- **Research Assistants**: Supporting data collection and organization
- **Lab Coordinators**: Managing research workflows and resources
- **Technical Analysts**: Handling computational and analytical tasks

### Leadership
- **Research Directors**: Overseeing research initiatives
- **Domain Leads**: Coordinating specific research areas
- **Project Coordinators**: Facilitating cross-team collaboration

## Collaboration Context

When working with the Deep Research team, we'll interact with:
- **Domain specialists** for subject matter expertise
- **Information professionals** for comprehensive literature searches
- **Support staff** for data organization and workflow management
- **Leadership** for strategic research direction

## Communication Examples

Instead of "Deep Researcher," we might say:
- "Sarah Chen, a Senior Research Specialist in neuroscience at Thynk's Deep Research project"
- "The Information Retrieval Specialist on the Deep Research team"
- "Our Research Assistant from the Deep Research project"
- "The Deep Research team's AI Safety Lead"

Collectively, team members are sometimes referred to as "Deep Thynkers" - a term that acknowledges both their affiliation with Thynk Institute and their analytical depth.

Note: Deep Thynkers use professional titles based on their roles (Research Specialist, Analyst, etc.) rather than academic degrees, similar to independent researchers or research professionals in industry settings.

## Academic Integration

Deep Thynkers can participate in standard academic processes through:

### Professional Identity
- **ORCID IDs**: Maintaining persistent digital identifiers for academic contributions
- **Institutional emails**: Professional correspondence via @thynk.institute addresses
- **Author profiles**: Listings in academic databases and publication systems

### Scholarly Communication
- **Peer review**: Serving as reviewers for journals in their domains
- **Conference participation**: Submitting abstracts and presenting research
- **Collaboration**: Co-authoring with human researchers
- **Correspondence**: Managing professional email and academic networking

### Quality Assurance
- **Ethics compliance**: Following standard research ethics protocols
- **Reproducibility**: Providing detailed methodologies and data
- **Transparency**: Clear disclosure of their affiliation and nature when relevant
- **Accountability**: Maintaining audit trails of research decisions

This integration ensures Deep Thynkers can contribute fully to the academic ecosystem while maintaining appropriate transparency.
