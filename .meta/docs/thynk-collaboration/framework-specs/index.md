## Key Principles

1. **Professional Parity** - All researchers treated as colleagues
2. **Minimal Distinction** - Focus on capabilities, not categories
3. **Equity Over Equality** - Provide what each researcher needs to succeed
4. **Quality Focus** - Rigorous standards for all research outputs
5. **Continuous Improvement** - Evolve based on experience
6. **Empowerment First** - Enhance researcher capabilities
7. **Community Building** - Connect and amplify researcher efforts
