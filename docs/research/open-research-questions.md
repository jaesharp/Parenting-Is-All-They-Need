# Open Research Questions for Collaboration

## Core Conceptual Questions

### What Makes Parenting "Work"?
- What are the essential elements that make parenting successful across cultures?
- How do we distinguish between cultural variations and universal principles?
- What parenting failures are most instructive for AI development?

### The Mathematics of Development
- Can we formalize "developmental readiness" mathematically?
- How do critical periods emerge from system dynamics?
- What makes some developmental trajectories more robust than others?

### AI-Specific Challenges
- How do we handle the speed differential (AI development vs human development)?
- What does "attachment" mean for a distributed AI system?
- Can we define "secure base behavior" for AI exploration?

## Technical Research Questions

### Empirical Validation
- How can we test parenting principles in AI systems?
- What metrics capture "healthy development" vs just capability?
- How do we measure trust, autonomy, and cooperation in AI?

### Implementation Challenges
- How do architectural choices map to "parenting styles"?
- What does "setting boundaries" mean in terms of loss functions?
- How do we implement "natural consequences" in training?

### Theoretical Foundations
- Can we prove convergence to beneficial behavior under parenting paradigm?
- What are the theoretical limits of the parenting approach?
- How does this relate to existing alignment frameworks?

### From Sociology
- How do social learning theories apply to AI?
- What family dynamics emerge in multi-agent systems?
- How do we prepare society for AI-as-children framing?

### From Cultural Studies

- How do we avoid cultural bias in "universal" principles?
- What can diverse parenting traditions teach us?
- How do we handle conflicting cultural values in AI development?
