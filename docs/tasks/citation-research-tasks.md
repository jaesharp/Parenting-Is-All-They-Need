## Research Strategy

### Parallel Search Approach
1. **Historical/Sociological** - Social science databases
2. **Biological/Physiological** - Medical/biology journals  
3. **Technical/Mathematical** - CS/Math repositories
4. **Interdisciplinary** - Cross-domain searches

### Quality Criteria
- Prefer peer-reviewed sources
- Recent reviews and meta-analyses when available
- Minimum relevance score: 0.8
- Flag controversial claims for special review
